Help & FAQ

Everything you need to know about customizing your dream blooms

To request highly personalized details (like custom initials, cards, or specific color schemes), simply hit the "DM to Order" link on Instagram with your Order ID, and we'll craft it exactly how you dream it!
We primarily craft our flowers using premium tactile pipe-cleaners, crochet wool yarns, felt, and lovely hand-selected tissue wrapping papers. They're soft, durable, and highly vibrant.
Because each piece is individually hand-crafted with intricate attention, please allow 3-5 days for creation and 4-7 business days for shipping. Please book well in advance for special occasions!
